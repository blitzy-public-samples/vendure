## 1. Story Title

**Report the availability of every prospective reorder line through the same read-only Shop API query, so that a Returning Buyer learns which items cannot be obtained at the quantity they want before any cart line is written.**

Short form, transcribed from the epic's story table without rewording so that the two files agree: **Preview availability changes before commit** [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§9.2 Story Table].

The unit of work the title names is one finished slice, and it is an **addition to an operation that STORY-001-03-01 introduces** rather than a second operation. The query already exists after that story; this story adds the availability fields to each entry it returns, resolves them through the platform's own composed stock call, and classifies each prospective line into one of the five states the parent feature fixes. **It writes nothing, it holds nothing, and it reserves nothing.**

Three things that could be mistaken for part of it are not:

- **The price half is STORY-001-03-01's**, including the operation's input shape, its error channel and its integer-money invariant. This story inherits all three and re-decides none of them.
- **The published payload contract is STORY-001-03-03's**, including the absent-versus-zero encoding and the staleness caveat that has to appear in the schema documentation rather than only in a ticket.
- **Resolving an unobtainable line — skipping it, reducing it to the quantity that is obtainable, substituting it or abandoning the request — is FEATURE-001-04's.** This story reports, and stops.

**One clarification the title cannot carry, and it is the one that decides whether this story is worth building.** The commit path this preview precedes does not treat every unobtainable line the same way. A quantity or stock shortfall is accumulated per item and reported alongside the lines that did succeed, but a variant that is disabled or soft-deleted makes the platform's bulk add **throw and abandon the whole batch**. Section 4.3 sets out the evidence line by line. That asymmetry is why reporting availability *before* the write is worth an operation of its own, and it is the argument this story leads with rather than files as a footnote.

## 2. Source Traceability

The label is a **quoted objective clause**. The clause is C4 [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§10.2 Objective-Clause Map], reproduced verbatim from the objective statement:

> "before they commit to a reorder"

Its origin is the objective statement itself, and the epic's clause map assigns it to two stories — this one and STORY-001-04-01 [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§10.2 Objective-Clause Map]. The pairing is deliberate rather than accidental: the adjoining clause C3, on buyers being made aware of price and availability changes, lands on STORY-001-03-01, so **the two halves of one sentence are carried by two sibling stories** and this feature is the only one holding a story against each [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.2 Contribution To The Epic].

Read the clause precisely, because it decides what this story is measured against. C4 is a claim about **timing**, not about outcome. It does not promise that a reorder will succeed, that stock will be held, or that the availability reported will still hold at the moment of the write — and section 4.3 is explicit that it cannot promise any of those. What it promises is that the buyer is told **before** the commit rather than after it, so the observable outcome is the presence of a per-line availability classification in a response that is returned while the cart is still untouched.

Two consequences follow, and both are carried into section 5 rather than left here:

- **A timing claim is verified by a state assertion, not by a measurement.** The verifiable outcomes are an exact availability token, an exact classification, an exact entry count and the demonstrable fact that the cart is unchanged after the read. This repository declares no service level, latency figure or business target, and the epic reports that absence as a finding rather than filling it in [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§2.2 Business Value]. None is supplied here, and none is needed.
- **"Before" is not "instead of".** Because the read confers nothing, the commit still decides. A story that read C4 as a guarantee would have to hold stock, and holding stock against an uncommitted intention is a write that this feature does not perform [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.10 A Point-In-Time Read Is Not A Reservation].

## 3. Precedent In This Repository

**PRECEDENT:** partial

The searched set is named so the claim is checkable rather than asserted: the whole of `packages/dev-server/example-plugins/`, the whole of `packages/dev-server/test-plugins/`, and `packages/dashboard/test-plans/`. That is the same set the epic searched for its nomination [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§9.6 Nomination] and the same set the parent feature searched for its own disclosure [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.12 Precedent In This Repository].

**Why the value is `partial` and not `none`, and why it is not `near-identical` either.** The verdict splits along one line: *reporting a variant's availability as a public string* is shipped, configurable and tested in this repository, while *correlating availability to the lines of a chosen past order or saved list, at the quantity the buyer wants, before any order line exists* has no analogue anywhere in the searched set.

- **Shipped, and therefore precedent.** Availability is already a published Shop API field, `stockLevel: String!` on the `ProductVariant` type [packages/core/src/api/schema/common/product.type.graphql:L56] and [packages/core/src/api/schema/common/product.type.graphql:L42]. The value comes from a deployment-configurable strategy whose single method is `getStockLevel` [packages/core/src/config/catalog/stock-display-strategy.ts:L27], declared on the interface [packages/core/src/config/catalog/stock-display-strategy.ts:L21] and documented as returning the string used directly in that GraphQL field [packages/core/src/config/catalog/stock-display-strategy.ts:L24-L25]. The shipped implementation is `DefaultStockDisplayStrategy` [packages/core/src/config/catalog/default-stock-display-strategy.ts:L14], and it is the source of the three familiar tokens [packages/core/src/config/catalog/default-stock-display-strategy.ts:L17-L21].
- **Also shipped: the numeric read and the display transformation this story consumes, in that order.** `ProductVariantService.getSaleableStockLevel` [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L323] returns the saleable **integer**, and the configured strategy turns that same integer into the display token [packages/core/src/config/catalog/stock-display-strategy.ts:L27] — the composition `getDisplayStockLevel` performs internally [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L362-L364]. **The integer is what this story needs and a string could not supply**, because `addableQuantity` is computed from it; an earlier version of this story consumed only the string-returning composed call and could not have produced the field it publishes [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L323], which reaches the stock layer through `StockLevelService.getAvailableStock` [packages/core/src/service/services/stock-level.service.ts:L72]. Nothing in the arithmetic is this story's to invent.
- **And tested.** A shipped specification sets the stock columns as database preconditions per variant [packages/core/e2e/stock-control.e2e-spec.ts:L595-L611] and then asserts the exact display strings a product's variants report, in `it('stockLevel uses DefaultStockDisplayStrategy')` [packages/core/e2e/stock-control.e2e-spec.ts:L618-L627]. A second specification covers the multi-location arrangement [packages/core/e2e/stock-control-multi-location.e2e-spec.ts:L34]. **This story's end-to-end work is written against that pattern and does not invent one.**
- **Not shipped anywhere in the searched set: a batch, source-correlated, pre-commit availability read.** The closest analogue for the shape of a customer-scoped Shop API addition is the wishlist example plugin, and its entire surface is three operations — `activeCustomerWishlist`, `addToWishlist(productVariantId: ID!)` and `removeFromWishlist(itemId: ID!)` [packages/dev-server/example-plugins/wishlist-plugin/api/api-extensions.ts:L12-L19]. **It carries no stock field and no price field**, so a buyer holding a saved list there cannot learn whether anything on it is obtainable. What this story borrows from it is one mechanism and nothing else: the `extend type Query` idiom for adding an operation without touching an existing one [packages/dev-server/example-plugins/wishlist-plugin/api/api-extensions.ts:L12-L14].

### 3.1 The Do-Not-Duplicate Boundary, And The One Sentence Of Novelty

The epic's inventory forbids rebuilding the price mechanisms and forbids bypassing the stock ones [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§5. Existing Mechanisms That Must Not Be Duplicated]. The price half of that boundary is inherited from the sibling story rather than re-argued: the in-cart, post-add change is already reported by the calculated getters `unitPriceChangeSinceAdded` and `unitPriceWithTaxChangeSinceAdded` [packages/core/src/entity/order-line/order-line.entity.ts:L163-L187], and the decision about which price wins after an add is already a deployment-owned configuration point, `handlePriceChange` [packages/core/src/config/order/changed-price-handling-strategy.ts:L32]. **Only the before-commit read is new**, and the parent feature proves that with a shipped specification rather than asserting it [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.3 Why This Is Not Already Solved].

**The one sentence of novelty: the shipped availability field answers "is this variant in stock" one variant at a time and knows nothing about a quantity, a source or a cart, and this story answers "which lines of the source I chose can I obtain at the quantities I want, in the cart I already have".**

Everything else follows from that sentence. The existing field takes no quantity argument [packages/core/src/api/schema/common/product.type.graphql:L56]; the saleable computation behind it is per variant [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L323]; and the stock layer beneath it resolves one variant at a time before delegating to the configured location strategy [packages/core/src/service/services/stock-level.service.ts:L72] and [packages/core/src/service/services/stock-level.service.ts:L79]. Correlation across a set of lines, at a requested quantity, against the buyer's own active order, is the residue — and the parent feature settles the arithmetic of it so that this story consumes a rule rather than choosing one [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.7a The Preview Is Computed Against The Target Cart].

**The unflattering half of this disclosure is required output, so it is stated plainly.** Nothing about reading stock is novel here. The three tokens, the threshold arithmetic, the untracked short-circuit, the location strategy and the test fixture that sets four stock columns all exist in this checkout already, and a reviewer holding the shipped specification would reject any wording implying otherwise. What this story adds is the correlation and the timing. That is narrow, it is stated narrowly, and it is enough — because the commit path's asymmetric treatment of a withdrawn variant, set out in section 4.3, means a buyer who is not told beforehand loses the whole batch rather than one line.

## 4. User Story

The three mandated lines are rendered as a fenced plain-text block so that each of WHO, WHAT and WHY occupies its own visible line when the file is read on any renderer and when it is pasted into an external tracker. **Every story file in this set uses that same convention**, so a reader comparing two stories compares like with like.

```text
As a Returning Buyer,
I want to see, for every line of a past order or saved list I am about to reorder, whether I can still obtain it at the quantity I want,
So that I learn which items have gone out of stock or been withdrawn before I commit the reorder, instead of losing the whole basket at the moment I submit it.
```

### 4.1 INVEST Criteria

- **Independent in value, dependent in merge order — and the two are stated separately because conflating them is what produced a contradiction in an earlier version of this story, which claimed no prerequisite in this criterion and named STORY-001-03-01 as its only story-level prerequisite in section 8.** The dependency is real and singular: **STORY-001-03-01 publishes the `reorderPreview` query and the entry type this story adds fields to**, so this story cannot merge before it. What is independent is the *value and the work*: the two read different operands over the same already-resolved source lines, share no new state because this feature introduces none, and can be **developed** concurrently [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§4.3 Intra-Feature Story Order]. Independence is claimed at that scope and no wider: the feature-level dependency on FEATURE-001-02 is real and section 8 classifies it rather than leaving it implied.
- **Negotiable** — parts of the shape are open and parts are deliberately not. Whether an unobtainable line is ordered first in the response, and whether the entry carries the deployment's own display string alongside the machine-readable state or only the latter, are choices this story may settle either way. **What is not negotiable** is that availability is resolved through the platform's composed call rather than recomputed, that no field publishes a raw stock figure, that the five availability states are the parent feature's [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.6 Named API Surfaces], and that `stockLevel` and `productVariant` are nullable so a withdrawn variant can be reported rather than dropped, and that the read is documented as conferring no reservation.
- **Valuable** — it is the point at which a Returning Buyer learns that an item is unobtainable **while the cart is still untouched**, which is the clause C4 promise in section 2. Its value does not depend on FEATURE-001-04 shipping: a storefront can render "two of these five lines cannot be obtained" from one response and let the buyer edit the request by hand, with no resolution mechanism merged at all.
- **Estimable** — the computation, the tokens, the threshold rule and the untracked short-circuit all already exist and are cited [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L323-L324] and [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L336-L340], the nearest behavioural analogue is a shipped specification [packages/core/e2e/stock-control.e2e-spec.ts:L618-L627], and the query field it extends is delivered by the sibling story. The surface to be written is therefore bounded and countable. Section 9 carries the numbers.
- **Sized Appropriately** — availability fields added to one existing prospective payload, one saleable-level read taken once per distinct variant and handed to the configured display strategy, and one clamp against the buyer's active order, over **zero new tables, zero migrations, zero new error results, zero permission definitions and zero asynchronous work** [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.11 No New Table, No New Error Result]. It is not a candidate for splitting: separating the disabled-variant classification from the stock classification would ship a report that tells a buyer an item is available when the commit is about to reject the entire batch over it.
- **Testable** — every criterion in section 5 names one published GraphQL operation and asserts an exact availability token, an exact machine-readable state, an exact entry count, an exact error type name or the equality of two responses. Preconditions are database column values a test author sets, following the shipped specification's own pattern [packages/core/e2e/stock-control.e2e-spec.ts:L595-L611]. No criterion asserts a state that has to be judged, and none states a figure this repository does not declare.

#### 4.3.1 The Database Preconditions — And They Are Preconditions, Never Response Fields

Every column below is set by a test author or by seeded data **before** the query runs. **None of them is a field the Shop API returns**, and section 4.3.3 states that as a prohibition rather than a preference.

- **`StockLevel.stockOnHand`** [packages/core/src/entity/stock-level/stock-level.entity.ts:L40] and **`StockLevel.stockAllocated`** [packages/core/src/entity/stock-level/stock-level.entity.ts:L43], on a row keyed by `productVariantId` [packages/core/src/entity/stock-level/stock-level.entity.ts:L30] and `stockLocationId` [packages/core/src/entity/stock-level/stock-level.entity.ts:L37] under a unique composite index on the pair [packages/core/src/entity/stock-level/stock-level.entity.ts:L19]. **Read that index precisely, because it decides what a precondition may assume:** it is unique on the *pair*, not on the variant, so one variant holds as many stock rows as there are locations stocking it. How those rows combine into one answer is the configured location strategy's decision [packages/core/src/service/services/stock-level.service.ts:L79], not an addition a test or a plugin may perform.
- **`ProductVariant.trackInventory`** [packages/core/src/entity/product-variant/product-variant.entity.ts:L155] — a three-valued inherit flag rather than a boolean, which is why a precondition states which of the three values it sets.
- **`ProductVariant.useGlobalOutOfStockThreshold`** [packages/core/src/entity/product-variant/product-variant.entity.ts:L152] and **`ProductVariant.outOfStockThreshold`** [packages/core/src/entity/product-variant/product-variant.entity.ts:L144] — the pair that decides whether the variant defers to the deployment-wide threshold or supplies its own.
- **`ProductVariant.enabled`** [packages/core/src/entity/product-variant/product-variant.entity.ts:L53] and the soft-delete column **`deletedAt`** [packages/core/src/entity/product-variant/product-variant.entity.ts:L48] — the two states that make a line unobtainable irrespective of stock, and the subject of section 4.3.4.

#### 4.3.2 The Inventory Settings — The Channel Pair Is The Authority, The Global Row Is The Fixture, And The Divergence Is Escalated

**An earlier revision of this sub-section ruled that the global settings row governs and forbade this story from naming the Channel columns at all. That ruling is withdrawn**, because it promoted a pair of columns their own declarations annotate as deprecated [packages/core/src/entity/global-settings/global-settings.entity.ts:L32-L45] to the status of specification authority — a decision a ticket may not take on the project's behalf. What replaces it is the epic's ruling R6, in three parts [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§6.4 The Settled Rulings].
**This is the single easiest way to write a criterion that cannot pass, so it is stated as a ruling rather than as a note.** Every stock precondition in this story states its intended values on **the Channel-level pair, which is the normative settings surface**, and **mirrors the same values onto the single global settings row**, which is what the shipped read path consults — together with the variant-level fields above. **Both halves are mandatory**: stating only the Channel pair seeds values this checkout does not read, and stating only the global row demotes the requirement to match the implementation. That is the epic's binding ruling R6 and its collision C10 [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§6.4 The Settled Rulings], and the parent feature carries the same rule for every story in this feature [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.9 Channel Scoping, Language Scoping And Monetary Values].

- **The authority is the Channel pair.** Every availability precondition in section 5 names `Channel.trackInventory` [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L100] and `Channel.outOfStockThreshold` [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L108] on the channel the request's token resolves to, because those are the values the specification this ticket set is built to names as live and the ones the `GlobalSettings` deprecation points at.
- **The single global settings row is seeded consistently with them, and that is a stated fixture-determinism requirement rather than a second authority.** It is required because it is what the running code reads, so a fixture that set only the Channel pair could leave inventory untracked and let a criterion pass or fail for the wrong reason. On a seeded checkout the two agree without any extra step, both pairs carrying the same column defaults of true and zero [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L99] and [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L107] against [packages/core/src/entity/global-settings/global-settings.entity.ts:L34-L35] and [packages/core/src/entity/global-settings/global-settings.entity.ts:L44-L45].
- **The divergence is escalated, not settled here.** Which surface *ought* to govern is architectural decision 5 in the epic — reconciled upstream in core, or the specification amended by whoever owns it — and it gates the **sign-off** of every criterion in section 5 that states a threshold rather than gating their design [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§8.2 Architectural Decisions]. **While it is open, a green criterion here proves only that both surfaces were seeded consistently**, and this story says so rather than implying more.

What the running code reads is checkable in this checkout, and is recorded as a fact rather than as an authority:

- `getSaleableStockLevel` destructures `outOfStockThreshold` and `trackInventory` from the **global settings service** [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L323-L324], and its private threshold helper reads the same source [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L344].
- That service returns **one** row, ordered by creation date and cached per request, with no channel predicate of any kind [packages/core/src/service/services/global-settings.service.ts:L63-L75].
- Even the component whose name promises channel awareness resolves a variant's effective settings the same way, reading the global pair and combining it with the variant's own two flags [packages/core/src/config/catalog/multi-channel-stock-location-strategy.ts:L181-L195].
- **No read of either Channel member exists in `packages/core/src` outside the entity declaration itself**, so the deprecation runs ahead of the implementation. That is the discrepancy, and the epic records it [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§6.3 Repository Documentation Discrepancies].

**Two things no criterion in this story does.** It does not describe both surfaces as authoritative. And it does not assert that **writing a Channel column causes** the platform to clamp differently, because no code path in this checkout reads one — which is precisely the assertion that reads plausibly in a ticket and then fails in a test run.
**The documentation discrepancy this story references and does not follow.** `Channel.trackInventory` [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L100] and `Channel.outOfStockThreshold` [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L108] are declared as columns with their own descriptions [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L93-L98] and [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L102-L106], and the global pair carries `@deprecated` annotations naming them as the replacement [packages/core/src/entity/global-settings/global-settings.entity.ts:L32-L45]. **In 3.7.0 the annotation states an intent that the implementation has not reached**, and the asymmetry is worth naming: the two Channel columns carry no deprecation marker while the global pair does, so a reader following the annotations **alone** would seed exactly the values no read path consults — which is why the mirror exists rather than why the requirement changes. Meanwhile the `ProductVariant` comment directing a reader to the global settings for the threshold [packages/core/src/entity/product-variant/product-variant.entity.ts:L146-L152] is **stale against the requirement and descriptive of the live path**, both halves being true at once. The epic records all of this as one documentation discrepancy across three surfaces and corrects none of them in place, because this run is additive [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§6.3 Repository Documentation Discrepancies — Noted, Not Propagated]. **No criterion in section 5 sets a Channel column and then asserts an availability outcome from it**, and where availability must differ by channel the plugin computes the difference from the resolved request channel and section 5 says so.

#### 4.3.3 The Publicly Observable Outcome — One Token, And No Raw Number

The saleable level is `stockOnHand` minus `stockAllocated` minus the effective out-of-stock threshold, the effective threshold being the global value where the variant defers to it and the variant's own value otherwise [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L336-L340]. **Where inventory is not tracked the computation short-circuits to the maximum safe integer before issuing any stock query at all** [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L326-L331] — it does not fall through to zero, and section 5 asserts that behaviour rather than assuming a numeric level.

That level is then handed to the configured display strategy [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L364], and under the shipped default the outcome is **exactly one of three tokens**: `OUT_OF_STOCK` where the saleable level is below one, `LOW_STOCK` where it is at or below the strategy's `lowStockLevel`, and `IN_STOCK` otherwise [packages/core/src/config/catalog/default-stock-display-strategy.ts:L17-L21]. **`lowStockLevel` defaults to 2** [packages/core/src/config/catalog/default-stock-display-strategy.ts:L15], a figure declared in this repository and documented in the strategy's own description [packages/core/src/config/catalog/default-stock-display-strategy.ts:L9-L10]; using it is therefore a citation rather than an invented threshold, and it is cited every time it is used.

**A criterion asserting one of the three tokens names the shipped default strategy in its Given.** The field's published type is `String!` [packages/core/src/api/schema/common/product.type.graphql:L56], the strategy behind it is deployment-configurable through `catalogOptions.stockDisplayStrategy` [packages/core/src/config/catalog/stock-display-strategy.ts:L14], and the three tokens are one implementation's return values rather than an enumeration the schema constrains — so an unconditional assertion would assert a value a deployment is free to change [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.5.1 The Three Stock Tokens]. That is also why this story publishes its own `stockLevel` as **an unconstrained `String`** and reports its exhaustive `ReorderLineAvailability` enum beside it: narrowing the string to an enum would break a deployment that had supplied its own vocabulary, and declaring it `String!` would make a withdrawn variant's entry unrepresentable — the two decisions are separate and this bullet is about the *type* while section 4.3 settles the *nullability*. **The two fields differ in nullability and that difference is deliberate rather than an inconsistency** — core's `ProductVariant.stockLevel` is `String!` because a variant being read always has a variant to describe, while this story's entry-level field is nullable because an entry for a withdrawn variant has none, which is section 4.3's first nullability decision.

**The prohibition, and it is absolute.** No field this story adds returns `stockOnHand`, `stockAllocated` or an unbounded saleable level — the epic's ruling R7 [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§6.4 The Settled Rulings]. The reason is the platform's own: the display strategy exists **because** directly exposing stock levels over a public API is treated as a leak of sensitive information, while a storefront still wants some indication of whether a variant is in stock [packages/core/src/config/catalog/stock-display-strategy.ts:L7-L10]. `StockLevel` is not a Shop API type at all — its absence from the checked-in introspection snapshot is asserted executably by the local validator suite rather than by a locator, because an absence is the one claim a locator cannot express [schema-shop.json:data.__schema.types] and [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§11.10 The Local Validator Suite]. Internally the two raw figures do exist: `getAvailableStock` returns the on-hand and allocated pair [packages/core/src/service/services/stock-level.service.ts:L72] as the `AvailableStock` shape [packages/core/src/config/catalog/stock-location-strategy.ts:L18-L21], by delegating to the configured location strategy [packages/core/src/config/catalog/stock-location-strategy.ts:L61]. **They stay internal**, and consuming the composed call rather than the parts is what makes that a property of the call graph rather than a rule someone must remember.

**The one integer that is published, and why its bound is the whole of its justification.** `addableQuantity` is `min(requestedQuantity, max(saleable − alreadyHeldInCart, 0))` — the same clamp the commit applies for the same inputs [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.7a The Preview Is Computed Against The Target Cart]. Because it is bounded above by the caller's own request, asking for two and being told two discloses that at least two exist and nothing more. The disclosure level is no greater than the platform's own, since `InsufficientStockError` already publishes `quantityAvailable: Int!` on a public Shop API mutation [packages/core/src/api/schema/common/common-error-results.graphql:L50] and [packages/core/src/api/schema/common/common-error-results.graphql:L53]; the change is only that a buyer learns it in time to act. **An untracked variant therefore reports `addableQuantity` equal to `requestedQuantity` and never the maximum safe integer** [packages/core/src/service/services/product-variant.service.ts:L326-L331]. **The residual disclosure is accepted explicitly rather than left unstated, and it is accepted with its ceiling and its two controls named**, because a caller can vary `requestedQuantity` across repeated calls and read the answer back — which makes the field a request-bounded inventory oracle. The ceiling is the configured per-line maximum, since `requestedQuantity` cannot exceed it under the shared selection contract [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-02-reorder-from-order-history.md:§2.5 Named API Surfaces]; the first control is that an entry exists only for a variant the caller already bought or already saved, so the oracle cannot be aimed at an arbitrary catalogue variant; and the second is that the equivalent figure is **already** obtainable from this platform by calling the existing `addItemToOrder` with a large quantity and reading `quantityAvailable` off the returned error, which carries the clamped non-zero figure when the quantity was reduced [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L743-L748] and zero when nothing could be added [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L710-L715]. The parent feature records the acceptance once for the whole feature and this story consumes it rather than re-arguing it [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.6 Named API Surfaces].

**One combination is specified rather than contradictory.** Because the token describes the variant and the state describes the request in this cart, an entry may carry `stockLevel` of `IN_STOCK` alongside `availability` of `NOT_ADDABLE_OUT_OF_STOCK` — when the buyer's active order already holds every obtainable unit. That pairing is expected output, and section 5 asserts it rather than treating it as a defect.

#### 4.3.4 A Withdrawn Variant Is A Distinct Class — The Argument For This Story

`OrderService.addItemsToOrder` [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L654] is the commit path this preview precedes, and its own documentation advertises that it accumulates per-item failures and advises checking the returned `errorResults` array [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L645-L653]. **It does that for a quantity or stock failure and not for a withdrawn variant, and the difference is visible in the control flow:**

- A quantity, state or order-limit failure is pushed onto the accumulator and the loop continues to the next item [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L681].
- A stock shortfall is clamped [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L703] and, where the clamp reaches zero, reported as an `InsufficientStockError` carrying the available quantity before the loop continues [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L710] and [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L712].
- **A variant that is disabled or soft-deleted is resolved through a call that throws rather than returns.** The lookup requires `enabled` to be true [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L687] and `deletedAt` to be null [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L688] on an entity fetch that raises when no row matches [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L684], and a disabled parent product throws explicitly [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L693]. **Nothing is accumulated, so the whole batch is abandoned.**

Two rules follow, and both are asserted in section 5:

- **A withdrawn variant is its own availability class, `NOT_ADDABLE_VARIANT_UNAVAILABLE`, distinct from a stock shortfall.** Collapsing the two would tell a buyer "out of stock, try later" about an item that will never return and whose presence in the request destroys the rest of it.
- **The plugin resolves and excludes such variants before delegating to the platform's bulk add**, which is the rule STORY-001-02-01 already fixed for the commit path and which this story agrees with rather than renegotiating [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§6.4 The Settled Rulings].

#### 4.3.5 This Story Reports; FEATURE-001-04 Resolves

The boundary is stated explicitly so this story does not annex the next feature's scope and so that feature can consume this vocabulary unchanged. **Reporting** an availability token, a machine-readable state and a bounded obtainable quantity is this story. **Resolving** — skipping the line, reducing it to the obtainable quantity, substituting another variant or abandoning the request — is FEATURE-001-04, and STORY-001-04-01 is the story that acts on the states named here [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§4.2 Downstream]. No criterion in section 5 asserts that a line was skipped, reduced, substituted or abandoned, because this story performs none of those.

#### 4.3.6 Point-In-Time, And Not A Reservation

The availability reported here is a read taken at one instant and nothing more. **It holds no stock, creates no allocation, confers no entitlement and expires from nothing**, and no field may be named or documented in a way that suggests otherwise [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.10 A Point-In-Time Read Is Not A Reservation]. Between this read and a later commit, another order can consume the same stock and an administrator can disable the variant, so **a preview and a commit disagreeing is specified behaviour rather than a defect** — and the commit is the authoritative one, because it re-evaluates each line at write time [packages/core/src/service/services/order.service.ts:L703]. **No freshness window is stated, in any unit**, because this repository declares none and a stated window would imply a guarantee the read does not make. Section 5 asserts this inside a criterion rather than leaving it to prose, which is the whole point of stating it here.

#### 4.3.7 Channel And Language Scoping

Both are properties of the request rather than arguments a caller may omit [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§7.5 Request Prerequisites]. The channel is identified by a unique token [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L62] carried in a request header whose key defaults to `vendure-token` [packages/core/src/config/vendure-config.ts:L176-L184] and taken from the request at [packages/core/src/service/helpers/request-context/request-context.service.ts:L130-L131]; the language comes from a `languageCode` query parameter and otherwise falls back to the channel's `defaultLanguageCode` [packages/core/src/service/helpers/request-context/request-context.service.ts:L136-L145] and [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L74]. A source belonging to a different channel token does not resolve, and section 7 covers the mismatch.

Two scoping facts matter to availability specifically:

- **The channel restriction on stock lives in the configured stock-location strategy, not in the service method whose name suggests it:** `getAvailableStock` loads **every** stock row for the variant with no channel predicate and delegates [packages/core/src/service/services/stock-level.service.ts:L72-L79], and `MultiChannelStockLocationStrategy` — the shipped default [packages/core/src/config/default-config.ts:L140] — is what resolves each location's channels and keeps only those in the active channel [packages/core/src/config/catalog/multi-channel-stock-location-strategy.ts:L138-L161]. **`getStockLevelsForVariant` does carry a channel predicate** [packages/core/src/service/services/stock-level.service.ts:L56-L65] **and is not on this path at all**, so citing it for this behaviour is wrong; an earlier version of this story did. So the same variant can resolve to a different token under a different channel token, and section 7 states that outcome plainly.
- **The threshold, by contrast, is not channel-scoped in this checkout**, per section 4.3.2. A multi-channel deployment can therefore return a channel-resolved availability answer computed against a deployment-wide threshold. That is the platform's current behaviour rather than something this story introduces, and the preview reports it as it is rather than compensating — compensating would mean disagreeing with `ProductVariant.stockLevel` about the same variant in the same request.

**Nothing this story adds is language-varying.** Every field is an identifier, an integer or a machine-readable token, and the availability token is deliberately not a sentence, so there is no string for this story to own in any translation catalogue. Monetary values are not this story's subject; where one appears it is an integer in the smallest unit of its currency stated alongside its `currencyCode` [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L88], which is the invariant STORY-001-03-01 fixed and this story inherits.

#### 4.3.8 Duplicate Source Lines Project Onto One Key, And Source-Line Identity Survives Through The Inherited `contributors` Collection

**This story reports one entry per distinct projected variant, and that collapse is deliberate — but an earlier revision left it at the collapse and said nothing about what happens to the source lines that fed it.** Two facts make the silence a defect rather than a brevity. First, the commit path keys its own outcome report by projected variant and not by source line, so a per-source-line availability entry would report a different shape from the mutation it precedes [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-02-reorder-from-order-history.md:§2.6a The Keyed Line-Result Contract — How Identity Is Constructed, Not Assumed]. Second, the clamp has to be applied **once** to the projected key: two source lines each asking for three units of a four-unit variant must be told that four are obtainable between them, not that four are obtainable each.

**So the collapse stands and the identity is carried rather than discarded.** Three rules, all of them inherited from the parent contract rather than invented here [tickets/EPIC-001/FEATURE-001-03-price-and-availability-delta-preview.md:§2.6 Named API Surfaces]:

- **Each entry carries the `contributors` collection the parent declares**, one member per source line that projected into that key, in the source's own line order, never empty. A storefront that needs to show the buyer which of their source lines an availability answer applies to reads it there, so the collapse costs it nothing.
- **`requestedQuantity` on the entry is the sum over its contributors**, and the obtainable quantity is computed once from that sum against the settled inventory fixture and the buyer's active cart, per section 4.3.3's formula.
- **This story adds no field to `contributors` and duplicates none of its members onto the entry.** The monetary members of a contributor belong to STORY-001-03-01 and STORY-001-03-03; the availability fields this story adds are properties of the projected key, because a saleable level is a property of a variant in a channel and not of an order line.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

Eight criteria. **Each names exactly one published GraphQL operation under test**, so a failure points at a single contract. Criteria name operations, their arguments, and entity and column names as database preconditions; none names a resolver class, a file path or an internal method name, because those are not published contracts. The narrative in section 4 is not restated here — the story carries the value and the criteria carry the testable outcomes.

Four preconditions hold for every criterion without being repeated in each. Three are properties of the request rather than arguments a caller may omit [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§7.5 Request Prerequisites]: an authenticated session, a channel resolved from the `vendure-token` request header [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L62], and a language resolved from the channel's `defaultLanguageCode` [packages/core/src/entity/channel/channel.entity.ts:L74]. Each criterion still names its channel token and its language code, because an availability answer is meaningless without the scope that resolved it. The fourth is the deployment's configured display strategy: **every criterion asserting one of the three tokens names the shipped `DefaultStockDisplayStrategy` in its Given** [packages/core/src/config/catalog/default-stock-display-strategy.ts:L14], per section 4.3.3. **AC-5 is the deliberate exception to the first of the four**, since one of its branches removes the session on purpose.

**How the eight map onto the five required coverage classes.** Valid input is AC-1. Invalid or incomplete input is AC-5 and AC-6. An error naming the exact type returned is AC-6. Edge cases are AC-2, AC-3, AC-4 and AC-7. Every stock precondition below is written in the form ruling R6 requires and section 4.3.2 sets out: **the Channel pair as the stated authority, the single global settings row seeded consistently with it as a fixture-determinism requirement, and the variant-level fields as the overrides** [tickets/EPIC-001-reorder-and-replenishment.md:§6.4 The Settled Rulings].
